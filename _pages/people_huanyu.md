## Huanyu Zhou

Post-doctoral research fellow

I am a post-doctoral researcher at Prof. Zhu's group at BIT since Jul. 2026. With Prof. Zhu's expertise in AI-driven materials discovery, I am currently developing highly efficient free-energy methods for materials simulation, which play a fundamental role in understanding various properties of materials, such as phase stability and synthesizability. My expertise in various ab initio methods (DFT, HF, Gaussian, plane-wave etc.) and lattice dynamics (DFPT, finite-displacement, HA, QHA etc.), obtained from my previous research, would facilitate a relatively accurate benchmark.

I received a PhD degree in Chemistry and a MRes degree in Nanomaterials, both under the supervision of Prof. Nicholas Harrison at Department of Chemistry, Imperial College London. I also hold a BEng degree in Composite Materials and Engineering under the supervision of Prof. Weihong Qi at School of Materials Science and Engineering, Northwestern Polytechnical University.

For my personal life, I find cycling and badminton rather interesting, and also have a 90mm Maksutov telescope — but would rather spend most of my time indoor. I am generally a fan of history and classic Chinese literature, and particularly inspired by the literary history of Southern Dynasties.
